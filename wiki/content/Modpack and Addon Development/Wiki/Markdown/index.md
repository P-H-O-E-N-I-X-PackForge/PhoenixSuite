## Why markdown?
**PhoenixWiki's** **Markdown** support is something we are really proud of. **Markdown** was chosen because it is widely used
and helps show off information in a nice visual way when paired with Rich Text.

### What will be covered?
In this section we will teach how to use **Wiki's Markdown**, how to add more programming languages to its
code blocks, and how to use the engine outside the built-in wiki screen.

Currently, its support is focused on mainstream Markdown, as it excludes some features such as Mermaid and LaTeX. 
These will be added if sufficient demand for them appears, but as of now they were cut for scope reasons.

If you want to see any more information about **Wiki's Markdown** feel free to let us know on **GitHub** or the **Discord server**.
