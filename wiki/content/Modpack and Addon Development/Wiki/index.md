## What PhoenixWiki does.
**Wiki** is a library mod that I made for the **Phoenixsuite** to help reduce duplicated code towards **Markdown** rendering/parsing,
theming, and in game wiki for the mods.

### Who is PhoenixWiki for?
The *addon dev* docs of this section will be the primary focus.
That being said, there is still a lot of helpful information for packdevs included so would welcome you to continue reading. 
I would specifically look at the information geared towards the **Markdown** parsing/rendering.

### What will this section teach me?
The documentation in this section will be focused primarily on three areas.

1. How to make themes and some general guidelines around them.
2. What exactly is supported in the Markdown part of Wiki and how it can be extended.
3. How to author/use the in game wiki system as both an addon and packdev.

This section is unique in that its docs will diverge into other mods but still provide a bedrock to how those mods fit.
This is because Wiki is shared between many mods and some use it differently than others.
