## What this wiki intends to teach you.
The **PhoenixSuite** wiki is a compendium for information of every mod made or maintained by **Phoenixvine** and 
the community. It is meant to teach how to *use, interact, and play* with all or just a few of them.
These sections are all connected and work together to teach a whole but **can be read by themselves**.

We hope that by reading/working together with us on this wiki, we can build a solid ecosystem of mods
that keep on growing as we do.

## The sections and what they do.
There are *three major sections* all with their own focus. However, they can connect to each other when
information would be copied verbatim causing a duplication of information. 

### Gameplay
This is the section dedicated to showing *information geared towards the average player*.
This part of the wiki hopes to teach players how to play with the mods and surface level customization
to allow a solid understanding of the playing aspect of the mods.

### Modpack And Addon Development
This section is dedicated to teaching about how to use these mods as a *modpack dev or an addon dev*.
It delves into API, how to get started, how to customize on a much deeper level than player facing docs,
and how to create and iterate with the PhoenixSuite mods.
Most wiki maintenance effort will be put into this section.

### Development
This section dives deeper than the other two as it explains the underlying systems and code
that power the PhoenixSuite mods for *potential new devs*. It hopes to teach getting started working in the mods, 
where potential bugs might lie, explaining some design decisions, and sharing future plans with these mods.

## Why we maintain this wiki.

The **PhoenixSuite** wiki is a passion project driven by people who want to *become better at technical writing*.
**AI** will never be used to write these pages, it's use as a tool in the **PhoenixSuite** has it's line drawn here.

All curseforge/modrinth modpage descriptions are also included in the no **AI** rule.

Coming away from that serious note, this wiki is a place of using our abilities to hopefully help 
users of all amounts of experience enjoy and improve our mods. If you want to contribute or have advice
towards maintaining these systems *feel free to come talk to us* on discord or open an issue on one of 
the PhoenixSuite's GitHub repos.

## How to contribute to the wiki.
You do *not have to be skilled or experienced* in technical writing to contribute to the wiki. 
We enjoy working with people throughout many skill levels, and **we would be glad to work with and help you improve**!

Moreover, we are still in *early stages* and could take any help we could get.

This wiki uses **Material** and the **MKDocs** plugin to rapidly iterate and the steps to set that up for yourself
are included below.

1. Install Python 3.0.

2. Clone this repository.

3. Open a terminal inside the wiki/content folder.

4. Run the installation from requirements.txt command.

5. Setup a venv instance.

6. Activate that instance

7. Move back to the wiki folder via `cd ..`.

8. Run mkdocs serve.

9. Open your local site build via the link in the terminal.

Once you have completed these steps you can start writing pages and mkdocs will handle updating the site for you
so you can iterate, ask for some feedback, then submit a pull request!
